# The Knight's Kingdom

A 2D platformer where you fight off waves of hostile mobs, upgrade your character's stats, and try to survive as many days as possible. Every new day brings stronger enemies and shorter spawn intervals — how long can you hold the kingdom?

## About the Game:

Your goal is simple: **survive**. As days pass, mobs grow tougher and start spawning faster, forcing you to constantly improve your stats and adapt your strategy to stay alive.
